How to play

  • Drag or tap a gem stack from the tray, then drop it on an empty slab.
  • Matching gems tumble on their own. They pour off whichever stack uncovers another match underneath, so cascades keep going. If neither way uncovers anything, they flow onto the stack with the most of that gem on top, and on a tie onto the stack you just placed.
  • Before you drop, you see the result. While dragging, or after tapping a slab once, arrows show every tumble and the gem count it ends with. Tap the same slab again to place.
  • 10 or more of one gem on top vanish for points. Extra clears in the same turn multiply.
  • The number on a stack is how many matching gems sit on top.
  • Ledges at the back of the cave are higher ground. Each shows a gem and a target, like 2/6 Topaz. Build a Topaz run of 6 on a stack next to it and the ledge opens. The upper ledges can only be reached from the lower ones.
  • Foes act every few turns. The counter in the top corner shows when.
  • Prism gems are wild and match any color. Potion vials in the tray splash their color onto every stack they touch. The Potion tool recolors one stack's top gems.
  • Frozen stacks can't move until bursts next to them crack the ice. Shrouded stacks hide everything but their top gems. Fungus creeps onto empty slabs until a burst burns it. Rune gates link two slabs as if they touched.
  • The Deepwarden causes earthquakes. A rumble warns you two turns ahead, and the corner shows how big it will be. Quakes knock gems off stack tops onto neighbors, which can start tumbles.
  • The flames show a level's difficulty, from 1 to 5.
  • Clearing gems fills the chest meter. Chests give Undo, Shuffle, Hammer or TNT. TNT wipes the stack you tap and knocks 4 gems off each neighbor.
